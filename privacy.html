How information submitted to this website is handled.

Information you provide

You may voluntarily submit your name, email address, company name, country, service interest, budget range and project details through the contact form or by sending an email.

How enquiry information is used

Submitted details are used to review and respond to project enquiries, communicate about requested services and maintain relevant correspondence.

The contact form uses FormSubmit, an external form-processing service, to deliver submissions to the studio email address. Your submitted details are sent through this service and are also covered by that provider's terms and privacy practices.

Enquiry information should be retained only as reasonably needed to handle correspondence and potential project discussions. You may request correction or deletion of information you submitted by contacting us.
